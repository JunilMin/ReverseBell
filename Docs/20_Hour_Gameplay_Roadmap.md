# Reverse Bell — 20-hour gameplay target

## Target structure

- Floors 1–10: lower keep, movement and basic parry mastery
- Floors 11–20: barracks, mixed melee enemies and arrow traps
- Floors 21–30: foundry, armored enemies and rock traps
- Floors 31–40: clock tower, faster rhythms and combined hazards
- Floors 41–50: bell sanctum, elite encounters and final boss route

## Expected playtime

- First ascent and story encounters: 5–7 hours
- Weapon, health, and resonance upgrades: 4–5 hours
- Alternate rooms, optional elites, and side objectives: 4–5 hours
- Higher-difficulty clears and ending requirements: 4–6 hours

Total target for an average completion-oriented player: approximately 20 hours.

## Required future content

The current prototype establishes the 50-floor physical structure and combat
systems. Reaching the target requires authored room variants, five major bosses,
upgrade choices, checkpoints with persistent progression, optional challenges,
and save/load support. Floors must be selected from room pools rather than
repeating one platform pattern.
