# Vertical Tower Map

The current prototype contains a continuous vertical castle route:

- 16 connected climbing platforms
- 3 checkpoint banners
- camera follow with vertical bounds
- fall recovery at the latest checkpoint
- a bell chamber and ascent goal at the top
- floor progress shown in the HUD

All geometry is placeholder art. The layout is intended to validate jump distance,
camera movement, route readability, and the pacing between combat and climbing
before external art is imported.
