# Animated Knight asset license

Source: https://engvee.itch.io/animated-knight

Author: Engvee

The asset author permits use in commercial and non-commercial projects,
without a project-count limit, and permits modification of the sprites.

Imported content:

- Left and right idle/run animations
- Four directional combat animation sets, assigned by enemy archetype
- Block, impact, and death animation sheets

The original downloaded archives remain outside this repository.
